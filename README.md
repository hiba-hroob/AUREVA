# AUREVA

> **Luxury Salon Management, beautifully organized.**

AUREVA is a modern salon management system designed to bring appointments, clients, services, staff, and business insights into one elegant workspace.

Built with a strong focus on **clarity, simplicity, and premium visual design**, AUREVA turns everyday salon operations into a smooth and organized experience.


## ✦ Overview

AUREVA provides a central management experience for salon businesses.

Instead of treating the salon as a collection of separate tables and records, AUREVA presents the information as a connected experience where every appointment, client, service, staff member, and financial figure has a clear place.

The application includes dedicated experiences for:

* Dashboard & business overview
* Client management
* Appointment management
* Staff management
* Service management
* Revenue insights
* Recent activity
* Quick actions



## ✦ Core Features

### Dashboard

The dashboard acts as the salon's command center.

It provides a quick view of:

* Total clients
* Today's appointments
* Today's revenue
* Active services
* Weekly revenue
* Monthly revenue
* Most requested services
* Upcoming appointments
* Reminder appointments
* Available staff
* Quick actions

The dashboard is designed to give salon owners and staff the information they need without overwhelming them.



### Client Management

AUREVA keeps client relationships at the center of the experience.

The client area includes:

* Client directory
* Client search
* Featured client profile
* Favorite service information
* Total visits
* Total spending
* Client rating
* Last visit history
* Recent client activity

The goal is not simply to store client records, but to make every returning client feel remembered.



### Appointment Management

Appointments are presented as part of a clear daily workflow.

The system supports:

* Creating appointments
* Viewing appointment details
* Tracking appointment status
* Viewing upcoming appointments
* Daily schedule visibility
* Client, service, and staff associations
* Reminder-based appointment views



### Staff Management

AUREVA provides a simple way to keep track of salon artists and their availability.

Staff information is used throughout the system to connect the right artist to each appointment and provide visibility into current availability.



### Services & Performance

Services are connected to appointment activity and revenue.

The dashboard can surface the most requested services and display:

* Service name
* Appointment count
* Revenue generated

This gives the salon a simple business-performance overview without adding unnecessary complexity.

AUREVA is built using a web application architecture based around:

* ASP.NET Core MVC
* Razor Views
* C#
* HTML5
* CSS3
* Responsive Web Design

The application uses Razor-driven dynamic content to connect the interface with salon data and business logic.



## ✦ Project Philosophy

AUREVA follows one central idea:

> **A beautiful salon runs on beautiful organization.**

The system is designed around the belief that business software does not need to feel cold, complicated, or generic.

Every page should feel intentional.

Every number should have context.

Every client should feel remembered.

And every interaction should support the rhythm of the salon.



## ✦ User Experience

AUREVA focuses on reducing visual and operational noise.

Rather than presenting large amounts of information at once, the interface uses hierarchy to guide attention:

1. What needs attention now
2. What is happening next
3. What is performing well
4. Who the client is
5. What action should happen next

This creates a calmer management experience while keeping important information visible.



## ✦ Responsive Design

The interface is designed to adapt across:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Layouts, typography, grids, cards, navigation, appointments, and actions adjust at smaller screen sizes to preserve usability without losing the AUREVA visual identity.
